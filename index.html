Our Story

Born on the canal,
brewed in Centerville.

Lock 27 Brewing is an Ohio independent craft brewery, founded in 2012 by Steve Barnhart and named to honor the impact of the Miami & Erie Canal on Dayton.

The Centerville brewpub is where it all started. It is family owned and operated, with a focus on beers that are true to style and a kitchen that treats food as seriously as the brewing.

Today the Centerville space runs as Lock 27 Brewing Restaurant, built around food, cocktails and a lineup of Lock 27 brews.
